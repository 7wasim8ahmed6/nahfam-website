Overview

A focused sourcing partner for Indian green coffee and coco peat

NAHFAM Global Enterprises is a Bengaluru-based merchant exporter focused on Indian coffee and coco peat / coir substrates. We begin with the buyer requirement — product type, grade or treatment, process, quality, quantity, packing and destination — and then match it with suitable Indian supply sources.

Our Purpose

To connect genuine Indian coffee and coir sources with international buyers through clear specifications, responsive communication and order-specific supply.

Buyer-First Approach

We do not force a fixed lot. We first understand the requirement and then source the closest suitable available Indian coffee or coco peat lot.

Coco Peat Range

Indian coco peat, by category

NAHFAM Global Enterprises also sources Indian coco peat and coir substrates for commercial growers, importers and international buyers. Core coir categories for horticulture, growing media and distribution — final specification, treatment and packing are confirmed against the actual offered lot.

Coco Peat Product / TypeTypical Buyer-Relevant Specifications
5 kg Block — Washed Low ECTypical target EC ≤ 0.5 mS/cm (test method to be agreed), pH 5.5–6.8, expansion about 70–75 L/block, moisture ≤ 20%, 5:1 compression.
5 kg Block — Unwashed / High ECEC typically > 1.0 mS/cm, pH about 5.5–6.8, expansion about 65–75 L/block, moisture ≤ 20%; used for general horticulture, soil blending and landscaping.
Coco Grow Bags (Hydroponic Slabs)Washed / buffered substrate; buyer-defined slab dimensions and pith-chip blends; UV-stabilized sleeves with plant and drain holes as required.
650 g Coco BriquettesWashed low-EC grades available; compact retail / nursery format, with shrink-wrap, labels and master cartons as required; expansion confirmed lot-wise.

Packaging & Loadability

Floor Loaded

40 ft HC floor-loaded: up to about 26 MT is common for dense 5 kg block programs.

Palletized

Typically around 20 pallets and about 22–24 MT per 40 ft HC container.

Custom

Grow-bag dimensions, pre-cut holes, labels, cartons and private branding aligned with the buyer.

Actual loadability depends on block dimensions, moisture, pallet configuration, container payload limits and destination requirements.

Global Export Capability

NAHFAM Global Enterprises supports buyer-specific Indian coffee and coco peat sourcing across the world. Our supply network includes established South Indian coffee and coir sources and professionals with export experience. We are open to orders of any quantity, from trial lots to full container loads.

Coffee Export Routing

New Mangalore Port (Mangaluru), Karnataka is our primary planned route for Karnataka-sourced coffee. The final loading port is selected according to destination, sailing schedule, freight and container availability.

Coco Peat Export Routing

V.O. Chidambaranar Port (Tuticorin) and Chennai Port are our primary planned routes for Tamil Nadu-sourced coco peat. The final loading port is selected according to source location, destination, sailing schedule, freight and container availability.
