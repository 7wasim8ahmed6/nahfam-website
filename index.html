Overview

A focused sourcing partner for Indian green coffee

NAHFAM Global Enterprises is a Bengaluru-based merchant exporter focused on Indian coffee. We begin with the buyer requirement — coffee type, grade, process, quality, quantity, packing and destination — and then match it with suitable Indian supply sources.

Our Purpose

To connect genuine Indian coffee sources with international buyers through clear specifications, responsive communication and order-specific supply.

Buyer-First Approach

We do not force a fixed lot. We first understand the requirement and then source the closest suitable available Indian coffee lot.

Export & Commercial

Clear terms after the requirement is confirmed

Global Export Capability

NAHFAM Global Enterprises supports buyer-specific Indian coffee sourcing for Thailand and international markets. Our supply network includes established South Indian coffee sources and professionals with export experience.

Planned Export Routing

New Mangalore Port (Mangaluru), Karnataka is our primary planned route for Karnataka-sourced coffee. The final loading port is selected according to destination, sailing schedule, freight and container availability.

Pricing Approach

We provide current price indications against a confirmed requirement. Grade, crop, quality, quantity, packing, Incoterm, destination and market conditions determine the final quotation.
